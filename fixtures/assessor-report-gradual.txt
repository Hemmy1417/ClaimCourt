Instructed by: the insurer

Instruction
To report on damage to the stockroom ceiling and to the goods stored
beneath it, claimed as storm damage occurring on 14 June 2026.

Findings
The ceiling boards are stained over an area of roughly nine square metres.
The staining is layered, with distinct tide marks at three different
heights, and the plaster behind the boards is soft to a depth of several
millimetres. Mould growth is established across the affected area and the
timber batten above the worst of the staining shows rot along its length.

In my opinion this damage has developed over a long period - I would put it
at not less than eighteen months of intermittent water ingress through a
defective roof upstand. There was rainfall on 14 June 2026, but nothing in
the condition of these materials is consistent with a single event on that
date. The deterioration is gradual and the upstand has not been maintained.

The goods stored beneath show the same pattern: the cartons at the back of
the stack are degraded and partly collapsed, with old water marks, not the
uniform wetting that a recent event produces.
