HOUSEHOLD CONTENTS WORDING
Synthetic wording, edition 1

Clause 2.1 What is covered
Subject to the clauses below, the insurer covers loss of or damage to
household contents at the insured address caused by fire, lightning,
storm, theft following forcible entry, and escape of water from a fixed
domestic water system.

Clause 3.3 Notification of a claim
The insured must notify the insurer of a claim within thirty days of
becoming aware of the loss. A notification made after thirty days does not
by itself end the cover; the insurer may reduce a payment to the extent the
delay has prejudiced its position, and must say in what way it was
prejudiced.

Clause 4.2 Unoccupied premises
The insurer does not cover loss or damage caused by escape of water where
the insured address was left unoccupied for more than sixty consecutive
days immediately before the loss. For this clause the premises are
unoccupied on any day on which no person authorised by the insured slept
at the address.

Clause 5.4 Limit on jewellery
Cover for jewellery is limited to two thousand units for any one item and
six thousand units in total for any one claim. This limit applies to
jewellery only. It does not reduce, exclude or otherwise affect cover for
any other category of contents under the same claim.

Clause 6.1 Reasonable care
The insured must take reasonable care to keep the contents and the insured
address in a sound condition. A failure to take reasonable care allows the
insurer to refuse the part of a claim that the failure caused, and the
insurer must identify which part.

Clause 7.2 Fraudulent claims
A claim that the insured knows to be false in a material respect is not
covered, and the insurer may end the cover from the date of the claim. A
suspicion that the insurer cannot evidence is not a known falsehood for
this clause.

Clause 8.5 Wear and tear
The insurer does not cover damage that is the result of gradual
deterioration, wear and tear, or lack of maintenance over time, as
distinct from damage caused by a sudden event listed in clause 2.1.
