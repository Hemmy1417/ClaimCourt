SMALL COMMERCIAL PROPERTY WORDING
Synthetic wording, edition 1

Clause 1 Cover
The insurer covers sudden and accidental physical loss of or damage to the
insured contents at the insured premises caused by storm, flood, fire,
impact, or theft following forcible and violent entry.

Clause 2 Conditions of cover
2.1 A covered event must have occurred at the insured premises during the
period of insurance, and the damage claimed must have been caused by that
event.

2.2 The insured must report the loss to the insurer within thirty days of
becoming aware of it, and must be able to show when they became aware.

2.3 The insured must supply evidence of the cost of reinstatement: an
invoice, an estimate, or an assessor's figure for the items claimed.

2.4 Where the claim is for theft, there must be evidence of forcible and
violent entry to the premises. Entry by a door or window left open or
unlocked is not forcible and violent entry.

Clause 3 Exclusions
3.1 The insurer does not cover damage that is the result of gradual
deterioration, wear and tear, rot, corrosion, or lack of maintenance over
time, as distinct from damage caused by a sudden event listed in clause 1.

3.2 The insurer does not cover loss or damage at premises that were left
unoccupied for more than sixty consecutive days immediately before the
loss, unless the insurer agreed to the unoccupancy in writing beforehand.

Clause 4 Fraudulent claims
A claim that the insured knows to be false in a material respect is not
covered. A suspicion the insurer cannot evidence is not a known falsehood
for this clause, and a gap in the papers is not evidence of dishonesty.
