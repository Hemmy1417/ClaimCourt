DECISION NOTICE - CLAIM NOT COVERED
Claim reference: SYN-0074
Wording applied: Household Contents Wording, synthetic edition 1

We have assessed your claim for damage to household contents reported on 8
October.

Our decision is that the claim is not covered, for the following reason.

Ground 1, under clause 9.4 (flood and rising groundwater exclusion). The
damage was caused by water entering the property at ground level during
heavy rainfall. Clause 9.4 of the wording excludes loss caused by flood or
rising groundwater, and the claim is declined on that basis.

If you disagree with this decision you may ask us to review it, and you may
take it to an independent reviewer.
