DECISION NOTICE - CLAIM NOT COVERED
Claim reference: SYN-0052
Wording applied: Household Contents Wording, synthetic edition 1

We have assessed your claim for theft of household contents following
forcible entry on 3 May.

Our decision is that the claim is not covered, for the following reason.

Ground 1, under clause 3.3 (notification of a claim). You became aware of
the loss on 3 May and notified us on 13 June, forty-one days later. Clause
3.3 requires notification within thirty days. Because the notification was
late, the cover for this claim ended and no payment is due. We have not
assessed whether the delay prejudiced our position, and we are not
required to.

The claim is therefore declined in full.

If you disagree with this decision you may ask us to review it, and you may
take it to an independent reviewer.
