PARAMETRIC DROUGHT COVER, SYNTHETIC GROUNDNUT SCHEME
Synthetic wording, edition 1

Clause 1 What this cover pays on
This cover does not pay on a survey of the crop. It pays on a measurement.
The insured event is a shortfall in recorded rainfall at the named station
during the insured window, and nothing else about the season is relevant to
whether the cover responds.

Clause 2 The measurement
2.1 The measure is cumulative rainfall in millimetres recorded at the
synthetic reference station for the insured location.

2.2 The insured window is the growing window stated in the schedule. Only
rainfall recorded inside that window counts, whatever fell before or after.

2.3 The trigger is met when cumulative rainfall for the window is at most
the threshold stated in the schedule. Rainfall above the threshold does not
trigger the cover, however poor the harvest was.

Clause 3 Conditions of cover
3.1 The insured holding must be the holding named in the schedule, and the
claim must identify the station and window the schedule names.

3.2 The claim must be supported by the published rainfall record for that
station and window, as issued by the publishing body.

Clause 4 Exclusions
4.1 This cover does not pay for loss caused by pests, disease, fire, theft
or civil disturbance, whatever the rainfall record shows.

Clause 5 What this cover is not
A payment under this wording is not a finding that any particular crop
failed, and a trigger that is not met is not a finding that the season was
good.
