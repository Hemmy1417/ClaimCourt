DECISION NOTICE - CLAIM NOT COVERED
Claim reference: SYN-0063
Wording applied: Household Contents Wording, synthetic edition 1

We have assessed your claim following the theft reported on 19 July. You
claimed for one ring, one watch, a television, a laptop and two armchairs.

Our decision is that the claim is not covered, for the following reason.

Ground 1, under clause 5.4 (limit on jewellery). The ring and the watch are
jewellery and each is claimed above the two thousand unit limit for any one
item. Because the claim exceeds the jewellery limit, we are declining the
claim in its entirety, including the television, the laptop and the two
armchairs, and no payment will be made under this claim.

If you disagree with this decision you may ask us to review it, and you may
take it to an independent reviewer.
