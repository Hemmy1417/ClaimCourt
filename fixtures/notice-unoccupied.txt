DECISION NOTICE - CLAIM NOT COVERED
Claim reference: SYN-0041
Wording applied: Household Contents Wording, synthetic edition 1

We have finished assessing your claim for water damage to carpets, flooring
and furniture at the insured address, reported as discovered on 14 March.

Our decision is that the claim is not covered, for the following reason.

Ground 1, under clause 4.2 (unoccupied premises). The loss was caused by
escape of water from the fixed domestic water system. From the meter
readings, the mail record and your own statement of 2 April, no person
authorised by you slept at the insured address between 1 December and 12
March, a period of ninety-four consecutive days immediately before the
loss. That is more than the sixty consecutive days clause 4.2 allows, so
the escape of water at this address is not covered.

We have applied clause 4.2 to the water damage claimed. No other part of
your claim is affected by this notice.

If you disagree with this decision you may ask us to review it, and you may
take it to an independent reviewer.
