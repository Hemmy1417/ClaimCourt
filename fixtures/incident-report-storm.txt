LOSS REPORT - SYN-2041
Insured premises: Unit 4, synthetic trading estate
Reported to the insurer: 11 March 2026
Date the insured became aware of the loss: 3 March 2026

What happened
On the night of 2 to 3 March 2026 a storm passed over the estate. Wind
speeds recorded at the nearest station reached 97 km/h. A section of the
roof covering over the stockroom lifted and water entered the unit
overnight. The insured attended at 07:20 on 3 March and found standing
water across the stockroom floor and damage to the racking and to stock
stored at floor level.

The damage claimed is the racking, the stock at floor level, and the
stockroom ceiling panels. All of it was wet on the morning of 3 March and
none of it was damaged before that night.

Condition of the premises
The unit was in use up to and including 2 March 2026 and staff were present
on every working day in the preceding three months. The roof covering was
inspected in September 2025 and no defect was recorded.

Notification
The insured became aware of the loss on 3 March 2026 and reported it to the
insurer on 11 March 2026, eight days later.
